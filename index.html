🛣️ Safer Route Comparison & Advisory

Compare primary travel corridors between major Maharashtra hubs with speed, toll, and safety insights.

Option A: Yashwantrao Chavan Expressway

Recommended Speed Route

Distance: 94.5 km | Time: ~1h 45m | Speed Limit: 100 km/h

  • 6-lane concrete expressway with median barriers and emergency lay-bys.
  • Critical caution: Watch out for brake failure zones at Bhor Ghat (km 38-46).
  • Average toll: ₹320 (cars). Continuous CCTV enforcement in effect.

Option B: Old Mumbai-Pune Highway (NH-48)

Scenic / Two-Wheeler Route

Distance: 118 km | Time: ~3h 10m | Speed Limit: 60-80 km/h

  • 4-lane undivided in several sections with active local village crossings.
  • Permitted for 2-wheelers and 3-wheelers (which are banned on expressway).
  • Zero toll; higher frequency of minor intersection collisions.
